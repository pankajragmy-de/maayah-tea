Cancellation & Refund

At Maayah Tea (Makro Enterprise), we believe in helping our customers as far as possible, and have therefore a liberal cancellation policy.

Cancellations

  • Cancellations will be considered only if the request is made within 1-2 days of placing the order. However, the cancellation request may not be entertained if the orders have been communicated to the vendors/merchants and they have initiated the process of shipping them.
  • Makro Enterprise does not accept cancellation requests for perishable items like flowers, eatables etc. However, refund/replacement can be made if the customer establishes that the quality of product delivered is not good.

Refunds & Returns

  • In case of receipt of damaged or defective items please report the same to our Customer Service team within 1-2 days of receipt of the products. The request will be entertained once the merchant has checked and determined the same at his own end.
  • In case you feel that the product received is not as shown on the site or as per your expectations, you must bring it to the notice of our customer service within 1-2 days of receiving the product.
  • In case of any Refunds approved by the Makro Enterprise, it’ll take 1-2 days for the refund to be processed to the end customer.
