Patience is a Ritual

"At Maayah, we believe that the best things in life cannot be rushed. Our tea is an invitation to slow down and embrace the quiet rhythm of nature."

Our Origin

Founded by Milan Upadhyaya, a second-generation tea farmer, Maayah Tea was born in the fertile plains of Upper Assam. We are dedicated to preserving the traditional tea-crafting methods passed down through our family.

We work exclusively with our 25-year-old family estate in rural Dibrugarh, where the soil is rich and the air is pure. Every stage of our production is handled by local artisans who have tea-making in their lineage.

The Craft

Unlike mass-produced teas, Maayah leaves are never touched by heavy machinery. From the gentle plucking of the two leaves and a bud to the rhythmic hand-rolling that releases the essential oils, our process is purely human.

This "Microbatch" approach allows us to monitor the character of every single batch, ensuring that what reaches your cup is nothing short of perfection.
