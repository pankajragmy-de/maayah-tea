mAAYAH'S Philosophy

TEA AS A PRACTICE OF PATIENCE

At Maayah Tea, we believe tea is not meant to be rushed. It is a quiet ritual, a moment to pause, breathe, and return to yourself. Every leaf we choose reflects our respect for time, nature, and honest craftsmanship.

We work slowly and intentionally, allowing character to develop naturally, without force or excess. A simple cup, made extraordinary through care.

The Founder's Journey

Milan Upadhyaya is a second-generation tea farmer from Assam, carrying forward a 25-year-old family garden while quietly redefining its path. Growing up around a tea garden created both familiarity and curiosity, a desire to explore specialty tea making beyond conventional production.

Seeking a different perspective, Milan spent time on tea farms in Japan, learning discipline and craftsmanship alongside Japanese producers. The experience reframed tea as a form of art, revealing its endless creative possibilities.

Upon returning to Assam, he began experimenting with small batches, refining processing techniques, and perfecting tea craftsmanship. Through Maayah Tea, his aim is to reinterpret Assam tea through a specialty-driven perspective, allowing origin and craft to define each tea.

Rooted in Assam

Born in the misty gardens of Assam, Maayah Tea carries the rhythm of the land it comes from. Each leaf is shaped by slow mornings, fertile soil, and generations of quiet knowledge grown not in haste, but in harmony with nature.
